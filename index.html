About Me

Hi, I'm Sangam Sherestha, a Developer, Owner of YaarSangam Pvt. Ltd., and Tech Blogger from Bharatpur, Nepal. I have a deep passion for creating beautiful and functional user interfaces, specializing in App development and Web design.

With over 50+ web projects built, I have extensive experience in PYTHON, JAVA, PHP, RUBY, FLUTTER, SQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, jQuery, and Tailwind CSS. While I have some backend knowledge in PHP and Python, my main focus is on crafting modern, user-friendly, and interactive web experiences.

Additionally, I have created 15+ Android apps using FLUTTER and have a strong understanding of UI/UX principles, prototyping, and design tools like Figma and Framer.

Beyond coding and design, I run a tech blog where I have published 40+ articles on web technologies, UI/UX trends, and industry insights. I love sharing my knowledge and helping others learn about frontend development and design.
